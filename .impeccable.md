## Design Context

### Users

Task Horizon serves SiYuan users who manage tasks across documents, lists, calendars, boards, and review views. Users range from experienced task-system builders to newcomers who need familiar controls and clear outcomes without learning implementation terminology. The core job is capturing, planning, executing, querying, and reviewing distributed tasks in one operational workspace.

### Brand Personality

Calm, clear, and familiar. The interface should feel like a focused extension of SiYuan, with restrained, direct interactions and no competing visual language.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a compact, work-focused interface that follows native SiYuan controls, typography, spacing, and theme variables in both light and dark modes. Favor quiet hierarchy and progressive disclosure. Avoid marketing layouts, decorative card collections, oversized typography, gradients, glass effects, unfamiliar controls, and explanatory clutter.

### Design Principles

- Put the current task and user intent before product decoration.
- Reuse native SiYuan interaction patterns and theme variables whenever possible.
- Keep common actions direct, with advanced detail available progressively.
- Make write scope, consequences, confirmation, and results explicit.
- Preserve compact information density without crowding or hidden behavior.
- Preserve existing keyboard and mobile behavior and do not remove native affordances.
